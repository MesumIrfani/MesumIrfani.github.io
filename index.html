About Me

Islamabad, Pakistan

My research focuses on how autonomous vehicles decide where to go and how to get there safely: path planning in cluttered environments, trajectory optimization, and model predictive control with formal safety guarantees. I hold an MS in Robotics and Intelligent Machine Engineering from NUST, where my thesis built a full planning and control stack for unmanned surface vehicles.

Before robotics, I trained as an aerospace engineer (BS, Institute of Space Technology, cum laude), with coursework in flight and spacecraft dynamics and control. For almost five years at NaqCoDE Technologies I have worked on fixed-wing UAVs, and I now lead the propulsion team. That work keeps my research grounded in real vehicle dynamics, actuators and hardware limits.

I am seeking funded PhD opportunities in robotics, motion planning and control for aerial and marine autonomous systems.

Outside engineering I draw and paint, mostly Arabic calligraphy and graphite portraits. A selection is on the Art tab.

Obstacle Avoidance for Marine Robots

MS Thesis · NUST · Grade A

A hybrid global and local planning and control framework for unmanned surface vehicles in cluttered, dynamic marine environments, with safety certified at the control layer. Supervised by Dr. Khawaja Fahad Iqbal.

Architecture

  • Global planner: MCS-Connect, a bidirectional sampling planner with adaptive inter-tree magnetic coupling and an obstacle-proximity penalty.
  • Trajectory layer: B-spline kinodynamic optimizer with a clearance-preserving smoothing guard.
  • Local layer: COLREGs-supervised NMPC (acados) on the 3-DOF Fossen CyberShip II model, with high-order control barrier functions as safety certificates.
  • Perception and robustness: IMM multi-target tracking and a disturbance observer for waves, current and wind.
  • Validation: a 14,400-run statistically tested benchmark against RRT-Connect, Informed RRT* and BIT*, plus a closed-loop scenario campaign.

Research · Planning · Journal paper under review

MCS-Connect: magnetically coupled bidirectional path planning

A bidirectional sampling-based global planner for USVs in cluttered water. Two Sunlight-style trees grow toward each other under an adaptive inter-tree attraction, with an obstacle-proximity penalty that keeps paths away from walls. Tuned by Bayesian optimization, and benchmarked over 14,400 runs against the Sunlight Algorithm, Sunlight-Connect, RRT-Connect, Informed RRT* and BIT* on custom maps, MovingAI mazes and real archipelagic charts, with Welch tests and Cohen's d for every scenario. Current work extends the planner with formal completeness and optimality analysis.

Manipulation · Control

Liquid pouring with a ROT3U robotic arm

An application module inspired by liquid-metal pouring in foundries, one of the dull, dirty and dangerous jobs robots should take over. Inverse kinematics gives the joint angles for each waypoint, smooth joint trajectories are generated between them, and an Arduino drives the servos to pick up a cup, pour into a second cup and return home.

Pathology classification in phonocardiograms

Classifying heart-sound recordings as normal or abnormal using the PhysioNet/CinC 2016 dataset. Features such as zero-crossing rate, MFCCs and spectral statistics are extracted with librosa and fed to a gradient-boosting classifier.
